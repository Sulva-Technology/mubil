# Your website: a simple guide

This guide is for the Mubil Foundation team. It explains how to sign in, add events and news, and read messages. No technical knowledge needed.

## Signing in

1. Go to **mubilfoundation.org/admin**.
2. Enter your email and password, then click **Sign in**.
3. The first time you sign in, a short tour shows you around. You can replay it any time from **Help**.

**Forgot your password?** On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and follow the link we send you.

## The dashboard

The menu on the left (or along the bottom on a phone) has five sections:

- **Overview:** a summary of your events, news posts and unread messages, plus recent activity.
- **Events:** add, edit, publish and remove events.
- **News:** write and publish stories and announcements.
- **Inbox:** messages people send through the website's contact form.
- **Help:** short videos, answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

## Adding an event

1. Click **Events**, then **Add event**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**, for example "Community Health Day: Makoko".
   - **Date**, and the start and end time if you know them.
   - **Cover image:** drag a photo into the box or click to choose one. Any size is fine; the site resizes it for you. Landscape photos look best.
   - **Description:** what will happen, who it's for, what to bring.
   - **Venue** and **Address.** Add a Google Maps link if you have one.
3. At the bottom, choose **Draft** or **Published**, then click **Save**.

## Draft or Published?

- **Draft:** only admins can see it. Use this while you're still working on something.
- **Published:** it appears on the website within about a minute.

You can switch between them any time. To hide something without deleting it, set it back to Draft (or click the eye icon in the list).

## Writing news

1. Click **News**, then **Add news post**.
2. Add a **Headline**, a **Short summary**, a **Cover image** and the **Story**.
3. Use the toolbar above the story to add headings, bullet lists, links, or a **Pull quote** for a powerful line from someone you met.
4. Pick a **Category** (for example Stories, Education or Health) and check the **Author** and **Publish date**.
5. Choose **Draft** or **Published** and click **Save**.

## Editing or deleting

- In **Events** or **News**, click the pencil icon (or the title) to edit. Change what you need and click **Save**.
- Click the bin icon to delete. You'll be asked to confirm. **Deleting can't be undone**, so unpublish instead if you might need it again.

Your changes are backed up on your device as you type. If you close the editor without saving, you'll be asked first, and you can restore your changes next time you open it.

## Messages

- Every message from the contact form appears in **Inbox** and is also emailed to the Foundation's contact address.
- A blue dot means unread. Click a message to read it. It's marked as read automatically.
- Click **Reply by email** to answer from your own email app.

## Tips for good photos

- Use sharp, well-lit photos with people doing something.
- Landscape (wider than tall) works best for covers.
- Ask permission before publishing photos of people, especially children.
